Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

PoupaJá Portugal respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website or connect an authorized social media account.

1. Information We Collect

This static informational website does not require user registration, does not use tracking cookies, and does not sell or rent personal data. When an account owner authorizes social media publishing via OAuth (such as TikTok Content Posting API), access tokens are stored securely and used exclusively to upload and publish authorized video content on behalf of that account.

2. How Information Is Used

OAuth credentials and basic profile identifiers (such as account ID and display name) are used solely to publish scheduled or user-triggered video content to the authorized creator account.

3. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell, trade, or transfer user data to third parties. Authorized OAuth tokens are retained only while the creator's integration remains active and may be revoked at any time via the respective platform's security settings or by emailing us.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request access to or deletion of any stored integration data at any time by contacting poupajapt@protonmail.com.
